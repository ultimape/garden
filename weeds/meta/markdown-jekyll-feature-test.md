## 2. Blockquote callouts (notice box workaround)

Replacing the broken `.notice--warning` syntax with bold-headed blockquotes:

> **Safety note:** This was not improvised. The protocol was designed over a year, with $200 of monitoring equipment, four designated safety contacts, and a graded escalation ladder. Someone who tries to wing this without the preparation is not doing the same experiment.

> **Hedge:** This is a record of what one person did. It's not medical advice. Outcomes are concrete and measured. Mechanisms are inferential.

> **Important:** Predictions held up. That's a track record. It still isn't proof of mechanism, but it's stronger than vibes.

## 9. Long-form prose with mixed elements

The Citalopram withdrawal was the discovery engine. Trying to understand what was happening to me, akathisia[^akathisia], brain zaps, [[Lhermitte's-sign]], sent me into the genetics of drug metabolism. I had `rs7997012` (the G;G variant that makes citalopram 18% less likely to work) and didn't have `rs2032583` (the BBB transporter variant that lets antidepressants cross the blood-brain barrier efficiently). The drug was never going to work on me. The fact that nobody screened for these before prescribing it was the first sign that I was going to have to figure this out alone.

[^akathisia]: A movement disorder characterized by restlessness and inability to sit still. Common SSRI side effect, often misdiagnosed.
